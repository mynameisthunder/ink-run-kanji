# Third-party data

The dormant reference datasets in `jlpt-reference-data.js` and `frequency-reference-data.js` combine:

- Word ordering and readings from [Real Kana](https://realkana.com/).
- English definitions from [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html).
- Character meanings from [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/KANJIDIC_Project.html).

JMdict and KANJIDIC2 are provided by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group under the [EDRDG licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html), including Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 terms. Generated memory hooks and data assembly are part of Ink Run.
